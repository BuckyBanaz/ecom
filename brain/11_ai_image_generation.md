# 🤖 AI Lifestyle Image Generation

- **Objective**: Automatically generate in-context lifestyle images from a base product photo.
- **Plan Location**: `docs/ai-image-generation-plan.md`
- **Trigger**: "Regenerate AI Images" button in the admin product form.
- **API Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/ai/generate-lifestyle-images`
- **Technology**: Multimodal AI (e.g., Gemini) to place a product image into a new scene based on a text prompt.
- **Status**: Planned.
